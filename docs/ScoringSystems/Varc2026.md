<u>**10.1** Scoring Procedure - General:</u>
**10.1.1**
VARC scores each club event in the Spring Series, the Summer Series, the Fall Series, Shorthanded Series, a Division’s Championship Series and/or the Boat of the Year (BOTY) Series as listed in section 5 “Schedule of Events.”

**10.1.2**
All divisions will be scored in the same way in each of these series and as described in this section, **except**, if applicable, as described in section 10.2 Scoring Procedure Specific to Each Division, and according to their **division-sanctioned event and courses shown** in Table 1 and on the VARC website RACING page.

**10.1.3**
VARC scores each series as follows:
**10.1.3.1**	For each division, a boat’s Spring Series score will be the sum of the boat’s best 3 Spring Series scores.
**10.1.3.2**	For each division, a boat’s Summer Series score will be the sum of the boat’s best 3 Summer Series scores.
**10.1.3.3**	For each division, a boat’s Fall Series score will be the sum of the boat’s best 3 Fall Series scores.
**10.1.3.4**	For each division, a boat’s Division Championship Series score will be the sum of the boat’s best 2 Season Series scores (Spring, Summer and Fall).
**10.1.3.5**	A boat’s Boat of the Year Series score will be the sum of the boat’s best 2 Season Series scores (Spring, Summer and Fall) for all divisions combined.

**10.1.4**
If a host club does not use standard VARC division breaks, then boats will be scored in the division in which they raced at the event; VARC will not modify the results as posted by the host club except as follows:
**10.1.4.1**	When a host club has multiple courses a boat will be scored by VARC in the position in which they placed in their division on that course, as scored by the host club, except that:
**10.1.4.2**	For boats in a VARC division who are placed by the host club in different divisions on the same course, and with the same start time, VARC will use the corrected times for scoring each boat in its VARC division regardless of the division assigned by the host club, and
**10.1.4.3**	A VARC Division may stipulate which course(s) a boat may sail in as listed in division sanctioned event and courses  in Table 1 and the VARC website RACING page.

**10.1.5**
All boats racing at an event within the VARC division splits, including visiting boats, will be scored by VARC.

**10.1.6**
All events and series will be scored using a High Point Scoring System where first place in a division at an event will receive 100 points, second 99, third 98 etc. with the following modifications:
**10.1.6.1**	A boat that did not start, did not finish, retired or was disqualified shall be scored points equal to 100 less the number of boats racing in their division on the event. This changes RRS A4.2. 
**10.1.6.2**	If at any event a division has fewer than five boats racing, a boat’s score for the event will be reduced by 5 points less the number of boats racing in the division on the event. For example: 4 boats racing: 1st = (100-5) + 4 = 99, 2nd (99-5) + 4= 98, etc.; 3 boats racing: 1st (100 – 5) +3 = 98, 2nd (99-5) +3 = 97 etc.. 
**10.1.6.3**	A boat that does not register and a boat that registers but does not come to the starting area for all races on an event will be scored DNC and will receive 0 (zero) points for that event. This changes RRS A9.
**10.1.6.4**	For the Boat of the Year Series, if a tie remains after RRS A8.1 is applied, RRS A8.2 will not apply and the tie will stand. This changes RRS A8 Series Ties. 
**10.1.6.5**	The winner of each series will be the boat with the highest point total, and others shall be ranked accordingly.
**10.1.6.6**	As per 3.2.1, host clubs are strongly encouraged to use VARC Scoring (ORC for Div 0,1 & 2 and PHRF for Div 3 & 7) as these are the scoring systems the Divisions have voted to adopt.
**10.1.6.7**	If a host club does not use VARC Scoring in their NOR and SIs or ERs as per 3.2.1 but is able to provide courtesy scoring using ORC for Div 0, 1 or 2, VARC will score the races for those boats with ORC rating certificates. 
**10.1.6.8**	 If the host club does not use VARC Scoring in their NOR and SIs or ERs as per 3.2.1 and does not provide courtesy scoring under ORC for Div 0, 1 or 2, VARC will score using PHRF ratings and score all boats entered in the Division.
**10.1.6.9**	At least two boats must be scored in a Division at an Event for the results to be used for VARC BOTY and VARC division Championship Scoring.

## Implementation

Everything above this line is the specification, quoted from the club's NOR. Everything below
is how SailScores implements it.

- Calculator types: `ScoringCalculatorType.Varc2026Shorthanded = 15`, `ScoringCalculatorType.Varc2026 = 16`
- Recipe: `SailScores.Core/Scoring/Systems/TopX.cs` (`Varc2026`), used by both
- Tests: `SailScores.Test.Unit/Core/Scoring/Varc2026CalculatorTests.cs`, plus characterization snapshots `Snapshots/SharedSystems/*__VARC-2026*.verified.json`
- Base system script: `Sql Utility Scripts/Create VARC 2026 Scoring systems.sql` (VARC 2026 Shorthanded `3c9e5a71-2f4d-4b8e-a6c1-7d0e9b2f5a38`, VARC 2026 `8b2d6f14-9e3a-4c75-b0d8-5a1f7e3c9264`)

The two systems differ only in their settings: VARC 2026 Shorthanded has no minimum number of
boats and no "ties stand" setting. The status column compares the NOR with the code as it is
(2026-10-22): **implemented**, **differs** (the code does something else), **not supported**
(needs feature work), **out of scope** (not this system's job), or **question** (the NOR
doesn't say, or could be read more than one way). The club's answers to the questions are in
[Decisions](#decisions).

### How VARC series are set up

VARC scores events, not races: it takes each boat's place in its division at the event, as
the host club scored it (10.1.4). So in SailScores **a race is one event for one division**.
The race's fleet is the division, and the scorer enters each boat's place in its division
from the host club's results (or a code, rarely). Each season, set up:

| Series | NOR | Type | Scoring system | Fleet | Races or children |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spring, Summer and Fall, one of each per division | 10.1.3.1–3 | Standard | VARC 2026 | the division | the division's events in that season |
| Shorthanded | 10.1.3.6 | Standard | VARC 2026 Shorthanded | any competitors | every division's shorthanded events |
| Division Championship, one per division | 10.1.3.4 | Summary | VARC Division Championship (club system, below) | the division | the division's Spring, Summer and Fall series |
| Boat of the Year | 10.1.3.5 | Summary | VARC Boat of the Year (club system, below) | any competitors | every division's Spring, Summer and Fall series |

#### Once: the base systems (site admin)

1. Run `Sql Utility Scripts/Create VARC 2026 Scoring systems.sql` with `@AccessClubInitials` set
   to the VARC club's initials. Rerun it on a database that already has the systems: it updates
   their score codes to the club's answers (decisions 2, 3).
2. Site Admin > Shared Scoring Systems: check that VARC 2026 (Scores to count 3, Minimum boats 2)
   and VARC 2026 Shorthanded (Scores to count 3) exist and are open to the VARC club.

#### Once: the club's scoring systems (club admin)

The season and Shorthanded series use the base systems directly. The two championships each
need a club system, from Scoring Systems > Create a Scoring System:

1. **VARC Division Championship:** Parent Scoring System **VARC 2026**. Scores to count **2**.
   Leave Minimum boats and Ties stand after A8.1 blank: they inherit 2 and No.
2. **VARC Boat of the Year:** Parent Scoring System **VARC 2026**. Scores to count **2**. Ties
   stand after A8.1 **Yes** (10.1.6.4). Leave Minimum boats blank.

Don't add score codes to either one: the parent's codes apply, and summary series scores don't
use codes anyway.

#### Each season: the series (club admin or scorekeeper)

1. **Divisions:** a fleet per VARC division, with its boats (visiting boats included, 10.1.5).
2. **Spring, Summer and Fall series:** Create a Series, Series Type **Standard**, Scoring System
   **VARC 2026**, Fleet Competitor Filter the division. One per division per season series.
3. **Shorthanded series:** Series Type **Standard**, Scoring System **VARC 2026 Shorthanded**,
   Fleet Competitor Filter **-Allow any competitors-**. A boat only has results in its own
   division's events, so the other divisions' events add nothing to its total.
4. **Events:** for each event and division, add a race in the division's fleet, dated the event,
   in that division's season series (and in the Shorthanded series, for shorthanded events).
   Enter each boat's place in its division at the event. For ties, enter the same place for both
   boats (or TIE for the second), and skip the next place, as the host club's results do. Don't
   enter DNC boats: a boat with no result scores 0. Rare codes: DNS, DNF, RET, DSQ, OCS, UFD, BFD
   and DNE score 100 less the boats racing; SCP, DPI and ZFP score 0 like DNC; RDG scores the
   points entered in its code points.
5. **Division Championship:** Create a Series, Series Type **Summary Series**, Scoring System
   **VARC Division Championship**, Fleet Competitor Filter the division. In Edit Summary Series
   Properties: Child series scoring **Each child series is one race, scored by its series
   score**; Children Series the division's Spring, Summer and Fall series. Leave "Include child
   series scores of unranked competitors" unchecked (every boat in a season series is ranked).
6. **Boat of the Year:** as the Division Championship, but Scoring System **VARC Boat of the
   Year**, Fleet Competitor Filter **-Allow any competitors-**, and Children Series every
   division's Spring, Summer and Fall series.

#### What the results show

- A season series: each boat's best 3 events count; the others, and every score in an event
  with fewer than 2 boats scored, show as discards.
- A championship: each child series is a column scored by the boat's season series total. The
  best 2 count; the third shows as a discard. A boat that didn't sail a season series scores 0
  for it.
- Ties in a championship compare the 2 counted season series scores, best first (A8.1), not
  the event scores (decision 6). In Boat of the Year a tie left after that stands. In a
  Division Championship it goes to A8.2: the last season series' score.
- Minimum boats applies to the championships too: a season series column with fewer than 2
  boats scored would be left out. That can only happen in a division with a single boat.

### Recipe

```csharp
public static ScoringRecipe Varc2026(ScoringSystem system)
{
    var ranking = Ranking.By(new HighestTotal()).Then(new CountedScoresBestFirst());
    var tiesStandAfterA81 = system?.EffectiveSettings?.TiesStandAfterA81 ?? false;
    return new()
    {
        Name = "VARC 2026",
        RacePoints = new VarcEventPoints(),
        Formulas = FormulaSet.HighPoint,
        CodedResults = new CodedResultsOneBehindLast(
            TopXPoints.ForPlace, VarcEventPoints.IsRacing, keepManualPoints: true),
        FixedDefaultScore = 0m,
        Adjustments = [new RaiseToDefaultScore(), new NotAtStartScoresFixedOrZero()],
        Discards = [new RacesNeedBoatsAtStart(), new KeepBestScores()],
        Total = new HighPointSum(),
        Eligibility = [],
        Ranking = tiesStandAfterA81 ? ranking : ranking.Then(new LastRaceSkippingUncounted()),
        Glossary = new TopXGlossary()
    };
}
```

### Decisions

VARC scores are a whole event's results, so letter codes are extremely rare, and their handling is kept simple.

1. **Boats racing** (10.1.6.1, 10.1.6.2) are every boat with a score in the event other than DNC.
   A score shows the boat attended the event, which is enough. So a DNS counts: for 6 boats with
   4 finishing, a DNF and a DNS, the DNF and DNS score 94.
2. **Codes the NOR doesn't name** (OCS, UFD, BFD, DNE) count the same as a DNF, discards
   included.
3. **Penalties** (SCP, DPI, ZFP) are treated as DNC: 0 points, and the boat isn't counted as
   racing or scored.
4. **Ties at an event** (extremely rare): both boats score the points for the better place.
5. **The minimum of 2 boats** (10.1.6.9): an event with fewer than 2 boats scored is left out of
   all the championships, the season series included. "Scored" is any score but DNC (DNC usually
   isn't in an event's results anyway); a penalty is treated as DNC (decision 3).
6. **A8.1 in BOTY and the Division Championship** uses only the 2 season series scores that
   count, best first: a boat with a 1st and a 4th beats one with two 2nds. Event scores aren't
   compared. (Corrected 2026-10-22 after the club reviewed this page; the first answer was
   event scores.)
7. **Redress** (incredibly unlikely for an event): RDG scores the points the scorer enters. An
   RDG with no points entered, and RDGAve, score like a DNF (one behind the boats racing).
